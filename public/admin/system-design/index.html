Feature Toggles

JSON-LD Structured Data Injects Person + ProfilePage + TechArticle schema. Enables AI engines (Google SGE, Perplexity, Bing Copilot) to cite you as the answer source.
Sitemap Serves /sitemap.xml with all published articles. Allows Google to discover every article automatically without relying on crawl luck.
French hreflang (fr) Adds hreflang="fr" links so Google indexes French and English versions as separate search results. Only enable when French article content is ready.
Block all indexing (noindex) Adds noindex, nofollow to every page. Use only during maintenance. Removes the site from Google within days — hard to recover from.

AI Search Engines (GEO)

Generative Engine Optimisation — control how ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google SGE, Claude and Bing Copilot discover, crawl, and cite your content. These settings are the fastest-growing discovery channel in 2025–26.

LLMs.txt Serves /llms.txt — the emerging standard (like robots.txt for AI crawlers) that tells ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity what content to index and summarise. Increases citation probability in AI-generated answers.
Allow AI Crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot) When enabled, robots.txt explicitly allows GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, and anthropic-ai. Disabling blocks them, which prevents AI engines from using your content in answers.
E-E-A-T Author Signals Adds citation_author, citation_title, and citation_publication_date meta tags to every article page. These are the signals Google SGE, Perplexity, and ChatGPT use to attribute authorship and boost credibility (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness).
